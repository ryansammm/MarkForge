# Writing Principles

A few simple habits that make connected notes clearer and easier to navigate:

1. **Atomic Notes**: One concept per file.
2. **Explicit Wikilinks**: Use [[Welcome]] to connect relevant ideas together.
3. **Structured Frontmatter**: Keep titles and tags at the top.
